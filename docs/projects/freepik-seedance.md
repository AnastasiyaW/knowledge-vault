## What it is

Freepik, now Magnific, is a browser creative suite for creators who need Seedance alongside other video-model providers in one AI Video Generator. — Text, image, video, and audio references. — Camera and motion direction, multi-shot narratives, video extension, and native sound with lip-sync. — The full Seedance 2.0 model is native 4K; Fast and Mini top out at 720p, and Mini permits clips up to 15 seconds. choose the full model for a reference-led 4K delivery and Fast or Mini for previews; do not replace a 2.0 workflow with Seedance 2.5 until the live picker confirms it.

## How to use this

From 2026-04-02, practitioners evaluating Freepik for AI video should check whether Seedance 2.0 is available through Freepik’s generator and confirm the current access terms before relying on it.

1. Open the AI Video Generator, select Seedance 2.0, then set the aspect ratio and duration before generating.
  — <https://www.magnific.com/ai/docs/video-generator>
2. For text-to-video, state the subject, action, camera direction, and mood. For image-to-video, switch to Image, upload a reference, and describe the motion or scene; the image supplies the look and the prompt supplies the action.
  — <https://www.magnific.com/ai/docs/video-generator>
3. For a sequence, switch to Multi-shot, write one prompt and duration per shot, add up to six shots, then generate the assembled video.
  — <https://www.magnific.com/ai/docs/video-generator>
4. Use the full Seedance 2.0 model when native 4K is required; choose Fast or Mini for 720p iteration, and use Mini for drafts up to 15 seconds.
  — <https://www.magnific.com/seedance/seedance-2>

## Best practices

- Describe motion explicitly, then add camera direction, pace, lighting, and sound cues when native audio is wanted.
  — <https://www.magnific.com/ai/docs/video-generator>
- Keep a normal generation to one clear action or scene. Use Multi-shot for a short sequence; use separate clips and the Video Editor when transition timing or model mixing matters.
  — <https://www.magnific.com/ai/docs/video-generator>
- Use image references with start and end frames to hold composition and character detail across shots.
  — <https://www.magnific.com/seedance/seedance-2>
- Use only rights-cleared real-person references: ByteDance states that real portraits require identity verification or prior legal authorization.
  — <https://seed.bytedance.com/en/blog/official-launch-of-seedance-2-0>
